Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

JCA Studio is an independent personal website. For privacy questions, email jenna@jca-studio.com.

Information we receive

When you visit this website, the hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, requested pages, and request times to deliver and protect the website. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate website usage. We also offer optional Google Analytics, which is loaded only if you select “Accept analytics.” You can reject Google Analytics and still use the site. Vercel Analytics operates separately from this Google Analytics choice; its privacy and consent requirements are being reviewed.

Cookies and local storage

Your Google Analytics choice is stored in your browser's local storage so it can be remembered on future visits. If you accept Google Analytics, Google may use cookies or similar technologies for measurement. You can change your choice using “Privacy settings” in the footer. Revoking consent stops future loading on subsequent page visits; it does not retroactively delete data previously collected by Google.

External services and links

The website is hosted by Vercel and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Links to social platforms and email services take you to independent providers with their own privacy practices.

Your choices and rights

You may reject optional Google Analytics and contact us to ask about information relating to you, including applicable access or deletion rights. The exact handling and retention of data depend on the relevant service and applicable law.
