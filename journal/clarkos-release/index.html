Clarkos takes the family Argos catalog tradition online: guess the prices, compare answers, and compete for bragging rights. This is a web game, not an App Store download; open it in your browser to play.

What's included

  • Live multiplayer: start a game and invite family or friends with a joining link or code.
  • Phone-first guessing: each player submits their own price guesses.
  • Three rounds: six products per round, with prices in British pounds.
  • Scoring and leaderboard: closest guesses earn points, with a bonus for exact prices.
  • Shared-screen option: use the TV view to bring the game to the whole room.
  • Solo practice: try a game against computer players before hosting.

What we're testing

  • Joining and playing with relatives on different phones and networks.
  • How clear the setup, instructions, and scoring feel to first-time players.
  • Readability and performance on smaller screens.

These are testing priorities, not claims of confirmed bugs or fixes.

Open issues

No specific public-beta defects are listed yet. Issues discovered during Thanksgiving testing will be documented here as they're confirmed. As a beta, the experience may change before Christmas.
